# Scheduling staffing and coverage

Updated October 11, 2026. Use fictional records during current development/testing; the same workflow is intended for real daily hotel use.

1. Open **Scheduling → View / edit staffing needs**. Choose a position and **Property defaults** to save the repeating Monday–Sunday pattern. Enter whole numbers from 0 to 100 for each configured shift. Zero means no coverage required. Saving replaces that position's older staffing rules with the displayed pattern; other positions remain unchanged. Property configuration authority is required.
2. Choose **This week only** to change that week's requirements. A week is initialized from defaults when a property-wide scheduling editor first opens it, generates it, adds a shift or publishes it. Saved requirements remain independent of later default changes. **Reset to property defaults** explicitly copies the current pattern into this week. Neither action removes assignments; coverage above the target is called out. Requirement edits return the week to draft.
3. Click an employee's schedule name and **Edit employee**. Enable **Can work overnight shifts** only for approved employees. It starts unchecked and is independent of preferred shift. The sample overnight employees have it explicitly enabled. Shift templates have an overnight designation, and any assignment whose times cross midnight also requires eligibility. **OK with split days off** independently removes the consecutive-days-off preference/warning.
4. Choose **Generate draft**, or regenerate selected days. Unselected dates, protected employees and existing deliberate Manager commitments remain fixed. Generation respects hard eligibility and the configured scheduling maximum/rest. Desired hours and preferences guide allocation; the heuristic does not promise a mathematically optimal solution.
5. If selected dates have missing coverage, **Resolve missing shifts** opens automatically. Each gap shows date, position, shift times and people still needed. Choose an eligible employee, **Manager (general coverage)**, or **Leave unfilled for now**. Closing retains the draft; reopen using **Resolve missing shifts**. Counts and choices are recalculated after each decision.
6. Before assigning an employee, review the resulting weekly overtime/maximum-hour, newly split-days-off and rest/clopen warnings. Rest calculations include neighboring weeks and actual assignment times. All applicable warnings are shown together. An override requires acknowledgment and a reason; changing the candidate or times clears the acknowledgment. Unavailability, qualifications, overlaps and overnight eligibility cannot be overridden. The property settings expose separate scheduling maximum, overtime warning threshold (default 40 hours) and minimum rest (default 11 hours). These are planning controls, not payroll or jurisdiction-specific legal determinations.
7. **Manager** counts as a coverage commitment and appears in schedule views, history and exports. It creates no employee/account and is never inserted automatically. Because no individual is named, it makes no claim about individual overtime, availability or rest. Shared staffing and Manager records require property-wide scheduling authority; individual employee assignments retain scope checks.
8. Review quality and publish deliberately. Missing coverage remains visibly warned; it is not silently filled. Employees whose future overnight eligibility was removed must have affected assignments corrected before publication. Their shifts remain present, affected future weeks return to draft and their file explains the required correction. Historical copies are retained.
9. **Export schedule** produces weekly matrix, shift detail and **Staffing needs** sheets. The latter shows numeric required/assigned/missing counts for each day/position/shift. Manager details include actual times and overnight end dates; its matrix total reads **General coverage**, not an individual's hours. Exports follow actual account view/export permissions. Scoped exports omit shared demand and Manager entries outside their authority.

The public demo supports this entire scheduling workflow with fabricated records, in-memory changes and local XLSX generation. Reset or refresh starts again; it cannot save changes to the registered workspace.
